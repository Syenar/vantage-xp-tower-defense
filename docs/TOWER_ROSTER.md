## Unique functionality

**Ranger** is the adaptive generalist. Adaptive Rounds at level 11 changes behavior against armored, fast, and stealth enemies; Combat Mastery at level 15 turns every fifth attack into an enhanced adaptive round.

**Repeater** rewards sustained contact through Spin Up, multishot feeds, and Suppression.

**Cannon** controls groups through expanding blast mechanics, Shrapnel, Concussion, and kill-triggered Chain Detonation.

**Railgun** is the line-piercing armor specialist. Hypervelocity at level 15 lets kills along the beam stop consuming pierce capacity.

**Frost** progresses from slows to freezes, Brittle vulnerability, longer control, and Flash Freeze.

**Ember** applies persistent burns, spreads fire on death, suppresses regeneration, and gains Inferno area ignition.

**Arc** chains electricity between enemies, forks at level 8, improves downstream conduction, and gains Storm Circuit at level 15.

**Marksman** specializes in conditional precision damage: Weak Point, Patient Shot, Finisher, Elite Hunter, and Perfect Shot. It always detects stealth.

**Hive** uses autonomous orbiting drones. Faster Recall at level 5 refunds drone cooldown when a live target is lost and the drone reacquires; Hunter Logic distributes targets; Swarm Protocol adds a temporary five-drone burst.

**Beacon** is a support network: reveal, range, damage, projectile speed, reacquisition, and bounded Command Field stacking.
