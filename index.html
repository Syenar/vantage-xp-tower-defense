How to play

No manual upgrade button. Combat experience is the upgrade system.

1. Place towers

Choose one of 10 towers from the tray and place it inside a legal build area away from the enemy route.

2. Earn experience

Damage, control, and support contribution award finite experience. Towers automatically level from 1 to 15.

3. Reach milestones

Levels 3, 5, 8, 11, and 15 unlock major mechanics such as multishot, pierce, chain effects, stronger control, and support boosts.

4. Adapt targeting

Select a placed tower to target First, Last, Strongest, Weakest, or Closest enemies. Sell returns 70% of base cost.

5. Move the camera

Drag to pan. Use the mouse wheel or pinch to zoom. WASD and arrow keys pan on desktop; Home resets the full-map view.
